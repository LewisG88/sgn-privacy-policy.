Privacy Policy

How The Social Gaming Network collects, uses, stores and handles information when you use our social gaming app.

Effective: 12 October 2026  ·  Audience: Adults aged 18+

1. About SGN

The Social Gaming Network (SGN) helps adult gamers discover other players, create profiles, connect, communicate, join squads and communities, and organise gaming sessions. This policy describes our handling of information when you use SGN.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: Email address, authentication identifiers and information needed to operate your account.
  • Profile details: Gamer tag, display name, optional profile picture, gaming platforms, games, preferences and details you choose to share.
  • Social content: Friend requests, posts, direct messages, squad messages, community participation, gaming sessions and looking-for-group information.
  • App activity: Experience points, achievements, notifications, participation and other in-app activity.
  • Safety information: User reports and blocks, and information provided when contacting support.
  • Technical information: Information needed by our service providers to authenticate accounts, store data, operate the app and protect the service.

With your permission, SGN may access your device's camera or photo library when you choose to upload a profile picture. SGN does not require you to upload a picture.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide accounts and gamer profiles; support discovery, messaging, squads, communities and gaming sessions; send in-app notifications; operate XP and achievement features; respond to support and safety reports; maintain and troubleshoot the service; and process account deletion requests.

4. Information visible to others

Other SGN users may see information you make available through your gamer profile, posts, community participation, squads or other social features. Messages are intended for their relevant participants, subject to the app's implemented access controls. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public-facing content.

5. Service providers and external links

SGN uses Supabase for services such as authentication, databases, file storage and server-side functions. Supabase processes information to provide these services. If you follow a Discord invitation or other external link, the external service's own privacy practices apply.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. This statement describes SGN's intended practices and should be revisited if additional services or monetisation features are introduced.

6. Storage and security

We use authentication and access controls intended to protect account data. No online system is completely secure. Data may be processed or stored outside Australia depending on the infrastructure and service providers used.

7. Retention and account deletion

Information is retained while needed to operate SGN, subject to applicable obligations and technical limitations. You can request permanent account deletion in the app by opening your profile and choosing Delete My Account. You can also request assistance by emailing lewisgrima88@gmail.com.

Account deletion is designed to remove the authentication account and associated account data. When an account owner leaves a squad or community, ownership may be transferred to another member; groups without remaining members may be removed. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. The full deletion behaviour, including stored profile images, is still being validated before public release.

8. Adults only

SGN is intended for people aged 18 years and over. The app asks users to declare that they are at least 18, but does not independently verify their age. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may restrict or remove it. Contact us if you believe an underage user has registered.

9. Your choices

You can edit supported profile details, choose what to share, block users and request account deletion. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy enquiries, contact lewisgrima88@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when SGN's features or data practices change. We will publish the updated policy at this webpage and revise its effective date.
