Account & Data Deletion

The Social Gaming Network (SGN) — for adults aged 18 and over.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open SGN and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Select Delete My Account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm your deletion request.

Successful deletion permanently removes your SGN authentication account and associated records handled by the account deletion process.

Request deletion without using the app

If you cannot access SGN, email lewisgrima88@gmail.com with the subject SGN Account Deletion Request.

Include the email address registered to your SGN account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing your request. Do not send your password.

What happens to your data?

  • Your SGN sign-in account and directly associated account records are deleted when the process succeeds.
  • Profile photos are intended to be removed as part of the deletion process; this cleanup requires further verification.
  • If you own a squad or community with other members, ownership may transfer to another member. Empty squads or communities may be deleted.
  • Some content or records may need separate review, including records retained where required by law.

Data retention and assistance

SGN is currently in private testing. We are reviewing retention periods and deletion coverage. If you have questions about data that remains after deletion, contact us at the email address above.

For more information, read our Privacy Policy.
