The Social Gaming Network

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Our Commitment

The Social Gaming Network (SGN) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, and activities that facilitate or promote such abuse.

2. Age Requirements

SGN is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Users must confirm that they are at least 18 when registering. This confirmation is self-declared and does not constitute independent age verification.

3. Prohibited Conduct

Users must not create, share, solicit, promote, or distribute CSAM, engage in grooming or sexual exploitation of minors, or use SGN to facilitate child abuse or exploitation.

4. Reporting Safety Concerns

Users can report accounts and inappropriate behaviour using SGN's in-app reporting feature. Child safety concerns may also be reported directly to our designated contact.

Email: lewisgrima88@gmail.com

If a child is in immediate danger, contact emergency services. In Australia, call 000.

5. Responding to Reports

SGN's administrator can review submitted reports through a private moderation system. Reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated as serious safety concerns and assessed for appropriate action.

Depending on the circumstances, action may include restricting access, preserving relevant information where lawful and appropriate, and referring concerns to the relevant authorities.

6. Reporting to Authorities

SGN is committed to complying with applicable child protection and reporting laws, including making reports to relevant regional or national authorities when required.

In Australia, suspected online child sexual exploitation can be reported to the Australian Centre to Counter Child Exploitation (ACCCE): www.accce.gov.au .

7. Child Safety Contact

For child safety concerns or questions about these standards, contact SGN at: lewisgrima88@gmail.com .

8. Policy Updates

These standards may be updated to reflect changes to SGN's services, safety procedures, and applicable legal requirements.
