Account and Login Assistance

If you are experiencing problems signing in, creating an account or accessing your profile, contact support with your gamer tag and a description of the issue.

Never include your password in a support email.

Report a Bug

When reporting a technical issue, please include:

  • Your device model.
  • Your operating system version.
  • Your SGN app version.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Screenshots, where appropriate.

Community Safety

SGN is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful gaming community.

Users can report inappropriate behaviour and block other users through the app. You can also contact support directly regarding harassment, inappropriate content or safety concerns.
